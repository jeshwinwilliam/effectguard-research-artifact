# Independent Benchmark Curation Review

## Abstract

Assess whether an independent curator can transform externally sourced WfCommons
workflow topology into a sealed evaluation without treating synthetic
external-effect annotations as independently sourced facts.

## Materials

- `P4_EXTERNAL_BENCHMARK_CANDIDATE_MATRIX.md`
- `P4_SEALED_BENCHMARK_HANDOFF.md`
- `paper/P4_INDEPENDENT_BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md`
- `paper/P4_DEVELOPMENT_EVIDENCE_FREEZE.md`

## Questions

1. Are provenance and license records sufficient for chosen WfCommons graphs?
2. Which effect annotations require independent authoring or approval?
3. How should hidden outcome truth and invariants be sealed from developers?
4. What inclusion/exclusion rules prevent selection bias and leakage?
5. Is WfCommons suitable only for topology, rather than external-effect semantics?

## Reproduction and Deliverable

Inspect original workflow files named in `P4_SEALED_BENCHMARK_HANDOFF.md` and
compare them byte-for-byte to any proposed sealed copies. Return a curation
plan, exclusions, provenance concerns, and a benchmark-sealing recommendation.
