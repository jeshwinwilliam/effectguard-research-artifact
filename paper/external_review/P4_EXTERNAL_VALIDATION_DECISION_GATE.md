# P4 External Validation Decision Gate

## Before Finalizing an Independent Benchmark

- Curator verifies WfCommons provenance and license for every selected graph.
- Curator independently approves or authors effect annotations, invariants,
  inclusion/exclusion rules, and hidden outcome truth.
- Sealed inputs, hashes, access boundaries, and conflict declarations exist.

## Before Freezing a Confirmatory Protocol

- Correctness reviewer addresses safety assumptions and counterexamples.
- Statistical reviewer approves unit, pairing, sensitivity, multiplicity,
  missing-data, and stopping rules.
- Temporal practitioner and RETRO-informed reviewer assess capability mapping.
- The shared provider fault matrix is complete enough for claimed fault scope.

## Before New Experiments or Comparative Claims

- Curator-sealed benchmark and evaluator are available.
- Baselines receive equivalent provider capabilities and external fault inputs.
- Raw results, manifests, audit records, and analysis scripts are versioned.
- Protocol states negative-result and incomplete-pair handling in advance.

## Research-Paper Submission Gate

All prior gates must pass, confirmatory evidence must match the narrow claim,
and limitations must remain explicit. If novelty is judged overlapping or the
benchmark cannot be independent, choose NO-GO for a comparative research paper
and retain only an artifact, experience, or technical-report submission path.
